Welcome to my PCT scrapbook!
This way, when someone asks me, "oh, how was the PCT?" It's right here! These are a few pictures that I want to be able to show and tell.

Above: I went on a short side quest at Donner Pass Ski Resort (named after the Donner Party). There's a world of graffiti in there, some more sophisticated than others (see bottom right).

Top left: A dead shrub. It's my current background for my mac. I just like how the branches look like veins.

Top right: After 2500+ miles of hiking, we made it to Canada, all the way from Mexico! My trail family, The Royal Court of Queen Turbodiesel: Feather, Diesel, Milk, Calibri, McLOVIN, Tailwind (not pictured), Rip (not pictured). I am McLOVIN.
